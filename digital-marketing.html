Overview

What is Digital Marketing?

Digital marketing is the practice of promoting brands, products and services through online channels — search engines, social media, email and paid advertisements — using data-driven strategy and measurable outcomes. It is distinct from traditional/offline marketing because performance can be tracked, tested and optimised in real time.

What does a digital marketer do?

A digital marketer’s typical workflow involves planning SEO and content strategy, running Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns, analysing GA4 data, managing social media, automating email/WhatsApp marketing, and optimising conversions across landing pages and funnels.

How to Become a Digital Marketer — Step by Step

A structured path helps candidates move from foundational knowledge to job-ready capability.

  1. Learn digital marketing fundamentals and strategy: Funnels, audiences and KPIs.
  2. Build websites and landing pages: WordPress and conversion-focused page design.
  3. Develop SEO skills: On-page, local and technical SEO.
  4. Learn Google Ads and PPC: Campaign structure, bidding and optimization.
  5. Learn Meta Ads and social media: Audience targeting and creative formats.
  6. Build content marketing and email automation skills: Copywriting and workflow automation.
  7. Master GA4, performance marketing and complete a capstone: Analytics and live campaigns.

Timeline: 5-6 months of consistent study following this sequence provides a solid foundation for entry-level digital marketing roles.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the duration of the Digital Marketing course in Pune?

The program runs for 5–6 months. It includes 100+ live sessions and 165 hours of structured learning, with weekday or weekend batch options.

2. Do I need prior marketing experience?

No prior marketing experience is mandatory — the course starts from fundamentals in Module 1. Basic computer and internet familiarity is helpful.

3. What tools are covered in this course?

The curriculum includes 20 tools: Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, Google Business Profile, Google Keyword Planner, Meta Ads Manager, Facebook, Instagram, WordPress, Canva, SEMrush, Ahrefs, Mailchimp, WhatsApp Marketing/AiSensy, Looker Studio, ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Clarity and Ubersuggest.

4. Does the course cover both SEO and paid ads (Google/Meta)?

Yes. SEO is covered across Modules 3, 4 and 5, Google Ads and PPC in Module 6, and Meta Ads and social media marketing in Module 7 — with hands-on exercises and projects.

5. What is the capstone project?

The Live Projects, Campaigns & Capstone is a 20-hour module in which learners plan, run and present end-to-end digital marketing campaigns.

6. Are the classes live or pre-recorded?

Classes are live (100+ live sessions). Recordings are made available for later revision.

7. What is the fee for the Digital Marketing course in Pune?

The program fee is ₹17,500 + 18% GST, bringing the total to ₹20,650. The same fee applies in all modes and cities.

8. Are EMI options available?

Yes. EMI starts from ₹3,048 per month for 6 months.* EMI amount, tenure and eligibility may vary depending on the applicable payment or financing option.

9. Is there a demo or trial class before I enrol?

Prospective learners can request information about upcoming demo or orientation sessions through the admissions team.

10. Do you provide placement assistance?

Uncodemy provides Dedicated Career & Placement Assistance through a network of 850+ companies. Employment outcomes are not guaranteed.

11. Can I get an entry-level Digital Marketing Executive role after this course?

Entry-level Digital Marketing Executive roles typically fall in the ₹2.5–4 LPA indicative range. Actual outcomes depend on skills, projects, interviews and market conditions; no specific placement is guaranteed.

12. What is the salary of a digital marketer in Pune?

Indicative ranges appear in the salary table on this page. Salary ranges are indicative market ranges and are not guaranteed placement outcomes. Role, location, portfolio and specialization significantly affect compensation.

13. What job roles can I apply for after this course?

Typical roles include SEO Executive, Social Media Executive, PPC Specialist, Performance Marketing Specialist, Content Marketing Specialist and Digital Marketing Executive (Entry-Level).

14. Who is eligible for this Digital Marketing course?

Freshers, working professionals, business owners, career changers and content creators may enrol. Basic computer and internet familiarity is helpful; no prior marketing experience is mandatory.
